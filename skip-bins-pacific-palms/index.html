Last updated: 24 October 2026

Skip hire in Pacific Palms

Pacific Palms covers Elizabeth Beach, Boomerang Beach, Blueys Beach and nearby Smiths Lake, between Wallis Lake and the Myall Lakes. The drive from Forster runs through Booti Booti National Park. Many properties are holiday homes on bushy, sloping blocks, so skip jobs are often turnovers, renovations and big green-waste clean-ups.

Access on bush blocks

Steep or narrow driveways, overhanging trees and soft sandy ground can make delivery hard. Tell the operator about the slope, gate width and any trees, and ask about a smaller bin or skip bag if access is tight. Because of the distance from Forster and Tuncurry, delivery can cost more out here.

Nearest tip

The closest MidCoast Council waste centre is Tuncurry (Midge Orchid Road, daily 8am to 4pm) for cars and trailers. To the south, council also runs the Bulahdelah Waste Management Centre on Recovery Road, a transfer station with limited opening days that doesn't accept trucks with unsorted general waste. Check council's website before you go.

Council rules

A skip on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the road or verge, check with MidCoast Council first. See the permit guide.
